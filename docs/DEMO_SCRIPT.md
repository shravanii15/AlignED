# Demo video: narration script (about 1 minute 55 seconds, roughly 285 words)

Target length: 1:45 to 2:00. Portfolio and LinkedIn viewers rarely watch past two minutes. Keep the pace calm
(about 150 words a minute) and pause a beat after each click so the screen can be read.

Before recording: open https://aligned-shravanikulkarni.streamlit.app, let it fully load, set the browser to
100% zoom, hide the bookmarks bar, and close other tabs and notifications. Record at 1920x1080 if you can.

## How to make it, step by step

1. **Record the screen with no sound.** Use Loom, or the Windows Snipping Tool's screen recorder (Win+Shift+S, then the video
   icon). Follow the "On screen" column. Do not talk while recording; you add the voice afterwards, which is far easier.
2. **Make the voice.** Free options on Windows 11:
   - **Microsoft Clipchamp** (already in Windows 11): import the video, add text to speech, paste each narration paragraph,
     and choose a young American male voice (for example "Guy" or "Davis"). Place each clip at its time.
   - **ElevenLabs** (free tier) or **Microsoft Edge Read Aloud**: generate the audio, then drop it into Clipchamp.
   - Or use your own voice. A real voice usually feels more trustworthy to recruiters.
3. **Trim and export.** Cut the waiting time and spinners, export at 1080p, and upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Loom.
4. **Say it is synthetic.** If you use an AI voice, add "narrated with an AI voice" in the description. It costs
   nothing and avoids any appearance of presenting someone else's voice as yours.
5. **Add it everywhere:** top of the README, your portfolio project card, and the LinkedIn post.
